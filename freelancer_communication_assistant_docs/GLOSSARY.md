# Glossary

- FTA: Face-Threatening Act.
- Positive face: desire for approval, appreciation or social acceptance.
- Negative face: desire for autonomy and freedom from imposition.
- P: relative power between participants.
- D: social distance/familiarity.
- R: ranking/weight of imposition.
- Bald on Record: direct strategy with little redressive action.
- Positive Politeness: solidarity, approval or common-ground oriented strategy.
- Negative Politeness: minimizes imposition and respects autonomy.
- Off Record: indirect strategy leaving interpretation open.
- Avoidance of FTA: not performing the face-threatening act.
- Macro-F1: average of per-class F1 scores.
- IDOR: Insecure Direct Object Reference.
- CSP: Content Security Policy.
- CSRF: Cross-Site Request Forgery.
- PII: Personally Identifiable Information.
