# Annotation Guidelines — Initial Outline

1. Annotate the communicative act in context, not isolated keywords alone.
2. Distinguish explicit user-provided context from annotator assumptions.
3. Use `uncertain` when evidence is insufficient.
4. Mark P, D or R as `unknown` when not supported by context.
5. Label positive-face and negative-face concerns separately; both may apply.
6. Define a primary strategy for single-label experiments; retain secondary labels if mixed strategies are present.
7. Do not infer relationship power from gender, nationality, race, religion, disability or other protected traits.
8. Keep examples anonymized and record dataset provenance.
9. Resolve disagreements through adjudication and version the handbook.
10. Pilot the scheme before large-scale annotation; revise ambiguous labels.
