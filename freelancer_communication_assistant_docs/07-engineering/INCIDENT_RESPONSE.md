# Incident Response

## Severity
Critical: confirmed unauthorized access/exposure. High: exploitable vulnerability or compromise. Medium: limited abuse or disruption. Low: isolated configuration/policy issue.

## Process
1. Detect and record time, alert and request IDs.
2. Assign incident owner.
3. Contain: revoke sessions, rotate keys, disable affected endpoint/integration or isolate service.
4. Preserve relevant logs without copying unnecessary user content.
5. Assess scope and affected users/data.
6. Remediate and validate.
7. Notify supervisor/organization and affected users as legally/contractually required.
8. Restore safely.
9. Conduct post-incident review and update threat model.

Do not destroy evidence or make unsupported impact claims.
